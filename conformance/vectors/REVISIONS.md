# Corpus revisions

The corpus has one revision number, raised by every change that adds, retires or reclassifies a
vector. A vector's `since` field names the revision that added it. **A classification change is a
dated entry here**, in the revision that makes it, and never an edit to an earlier entry.

| Revision | Date | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-12 | The number area (milestone SJL-1.4): Number::toString at every radix with its argument edges, `toFixed`, `toExponential` and `toPrecision` with their argument edges and exact ties, `StringToNumber`, `parseFloat`, `parseInt`, the values of numeric literals, and array-index and canonical numeric keys. Every expected value is computed by `scripts/conformance/numref.py` (`ref:numref@1`) from ECMA-262 at `0248456c`. No vector is classed `divergence`. |
| 2 | 2026-10-12 | Classifies every difference of Broiler.JS's first engine-level run, on revision 1: 700 vectors become `divergence` with a `divergence.broiler-js` field. 595 are `Number::toString` at a radix other than 10: 233 longer than the shortest digits, 124 whose digits do not denote the value, 238 wrong at a magnitude of 2^63 or more. 105 others: 62 hexadecimal, octal and binary integers beyond 2^53 not rounded to nearest, ties to even, as literals and strings; 18 decimal significands beyond 309 digits that hang or give NaN; 7 `parseInt` inputs that trim U+0085; 5 argument edges of `toString` and `toPrecision`; 13 numeric-literal early errors not raised. The reasons, with the plans that answer them (Broiler.JS SharedLibraries items SJL-3 and SJL-14), are in `scripts/conformance/number-divergences.txt`. No expected value changed and no vector was added or removed. |
| 3 | 2026-10-12 | Classifies every difference of Broiler.VM's first engine-level run, on revision 2: 391 vectors gain a `divergence.broiler-vm` field. 343 are `Number::toString` at a radix other than 10: 59 longer than the shortest digits, 134 whose fraction digits do not denote the value, 150 whose integer digits do not at 2^53 or more. 48 others: 28 `parseInt` results not correctly rounded, 16 of them on `impl-defined` vectors whose `per-engine.broiler-vm` expectation (SJL-3.3) the engine does not meet yet; 16 hexadecimal, octal and binary integers beyond 2^53, as literals and strings; 2 radix-10 strings of 16 digits that do not round-trip; 2 readings of `07.5` as a literal. 359 of the 391 also carry a `divergence.broiler-js` field. SCHEMA.md section 5 now allows the field on an `impl-defined` vector, beside its per-engine expectation, for the 16. No expected value changed and no vector was added or removed. |
| 4 | 2026-10-12 | Adds 9 `parseInt` vectors, `numbers/parse-int/0375` to `0383`: three decimal strings of 21, 22 and 25 significant digits (2^67 + 2^14 + 1, -(2^68 + 2^15 + 1) and 2^80 + 2^27 + 1, the last with leading white space), each with the radix undefined, 0 and 10. Beyond 20 significant digits at radix 10 the clause admits every digit or the first 20 followed by zeros, and on these strings the two round to different doubles, so `out` lists both. Every long radix-10 string of revision 1 rounds to one double either way, so no vector held both options at that radix; bundle sjl-3-001's reference-arm comparison found 28 differences of this kind, between Broiler.VM's pre-cut-over `parseInt` and the shared library's, which its rule holds unclassified until a vector covers them. No vector was reclassified or removed and no expected value changed. |
| 5 | 2026-10-12 | Retires 696 of the 700 `divergence.broiler-js` fields: Broiler.JS's cut-over to the shared library (milestone SJL-3.5) corrects them, and its changelog, `Broiler.JS/CHANGELOG.md`, lists each correction. They are every field of revision 2 but the four of reason `strict-non-octal-decimal` (`numbers/literal/0082`, `0084`, `0088` and `0090`), which stay with item SJL-14. A vector left with no divergence field is `normative` again; one with a `divergence.broiler-vm` field stays `divergence`. `docs/evidence/sjl-3-001/engine/retired-divergences.tsv` lists the retired fields under their reasons. No expected value changed and no vector was added or removed. |
| 6 | 2026-10-13 | Adds the numerics area (milestone SJL-4.2): 12,493 vectors in eight files under `numerics/`, written by `scripts/conformance/generate_numerics_vectors.py`, every expected value computed by `scripts/conformance/numericsref.py` (`ref:numericsref@1`) in exact arithmetic. They cover the integer conversions (`ToInt32` to `ToUint8Clamp`, `ToBigInt64`, `ToBigUint64`, `ToIntegerOrInfinity`, `ToLength` and `ToIndex`'s range rule); Float16 encoding, decoding (every NaN payload) and `Math.f16round`; `Math.round`, `**` and `Math.pow` with their special cases in four script forms, `hypot`, `cbrt`, `expm1`, `log1p`, `sumPrecise`, `fround`, `clz32` and `imul`; the nine rounding modes through Temporal (`Duration.round`, `Instant.round`, `Instant.since` and `Duration.total`); and BigInt arithmetic, shifts and bitwise operations, at Broiler.VM's width ceiling and past it, its text at every radix, StringToBigInt with every white-space code point, `asIntN` and `asUintN` up to 2^53 - 1 bits, and its conversion to and comparison with Number. Where the specification leaves a result to the implementation (an implementation-approximated Math result, a Float16 NaN's encoding, a BigInt past Broiler.VM's width policy or past any representation), the vector is `impl-defined`, with each engine's expectation taken from its plan: Broiler.VM's JSD-0067 (decisions 2, 3 and 5) and Broiler.JS's `docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md` (SJL-4.2 and SJL-4.5); each note says which. `Math.hypot`'s expectation is one unit in the last place until the library states its bound, as JSD-0067 decision 5 has it. ECMA-262 at the pin has no Temporal, so the rounding vectors' provenance is the reference computation alone, which follows the Temporal proposal. No vector of the area is classified yet: each engine's first run on this revision does that, in the next. Retires the 391 `divergence.broiler-vm` fields: Broiler.VM's cut-over (milestone SJL-3.4) corrects every one, and its JSD-0066 section 4 lists the corrections; `docs/evidence/sjl-4-001/engine/retired-divergences.tsv` lists the fields under their reasons. 375 of those vectors are `normative` again and 16 stay `impl-defined`. No expected value of the number area changed and no vector was removed. |
| 7 | 2026-10-13 | Classifies every difference of each engine's first engine-level run on the numerics area, at revision 6. Broiler.JS, 54 vectors: 18 `BigInt.asUintN` results at widths of 2^32 or more, 9 cube roots that are not correctly rounded, 5 BigInt powers with an exponent of 2^31 or more, 5 BigInt property keys beyond 2^32 - 1 or below 0, 5 of `1 ** NaN` and of `1 ** Infinity` and `1 ** -Infinity` written as literals, 5 hypotenuses and 5 `expm1` and `log1p` results more than a unit in the last place away, and 2 hexadecimal StringToBigInt texts of 262,144 digits that do not complete. Broiler.VM, 100 vectors: 70 `expm1` and `log1p` results, 23 cube roots, 5 hypotenuses and 2 `sumPrecise` sums that flush a tiny term. Each reason names the cut-over that answers it, SJL-4.4 or SJL-4.5; the reasons are in `scripts/conformance/numerics-divergences.txt`, and the runs are retained in this repository's `docs/evidence/sjl-4-001/engine/` and in Broiler.VM's bundle `sjl-4-001`. Fixes one vector defect: `numerics/tostring-long/0016` built its 70,000-bit input as one hexadecimal literal, which Broiler.VM's wide manifest refuses (it admits no BigInt literal wider than 65,536 bits, a limit of its compiler and not of the area), so the vector pinned the literal rather than the text conversion it names; the input is now built from literals of at most 32,768 bits and shifts, and its expected value is unchanged. No other vector changed, and no expected value. |
| 8 | 2026-10-13 | Fixes vector defects the library-level runner found when `Broiler.ECMAScript.Algorithms` gained the numerics area (milestone SJL-4.3): a vector's `op` and `in` must decide its outcome, since they are all that runner reads, and 72 numerics vectors did not. The 16 that test whether a NaN is written as a NaN encoding are now `Float16 encode is a NaN`, apart from the 16 of `Float16 encode` that pin its bits for the same inputs; the 5 that pass `Math.sumPrecise` one argument rather than an array are `Math.sumPrecise argument`; the 12 that show the top 64 bits of a wide `asUintN` result are `BigInt.asUintN, top 64 bits`; the 23 width-policy vectors, which pin each engine's policy and give their operands as descriptions, are their operation followed by `(width policy)`; and the 16 long-text vectors give their value as `bigint:` rather than as a description of its width, the one that shows only the text's length and ends being `BigInt::toString, length and ends`. No script, expected value or classification changed, and no vector was added or removed. |
| 9 | 2026-10-13 | Retires every `divergence` field of the numerics area: the 54 `divergence.broiler-js` fields, which Broiler.JS's cut-over to the shared library (milestone SJL-4.5) corrects, and its changelog, `Broiler.JS/CHANGELOG.md`, lists each correction; and the 100 `divergence.broiler-vm` fields, which Broiler.VM's cut-over (milestone SJL-4.4) corrects, and its JSD-0069 lists the corrections. Of the vectors that carried them, 17 are `normative` again and the others stay `impl-defined`. `docs/evidence/sjl-4-001/engine/retired-divergences-revision-9.tsv` lists the retired fields under their reasons, and `scripts/conformance/numerics-divergences.txt` holds none. No expected value changed and no vector was added or removed. |
