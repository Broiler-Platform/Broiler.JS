# The number area's classified differences, read by generate_number_vectors.py.
#
# A line `reason <key>: <text>` defines a reason. A line `<engine> <key> <id>` classifies the vector
# <id> as a divergence of <engine> for that reason: the generator writes the vector with class
# divergence and the field divergence.<engine>: <text>. The vector's expected value never changes;
# a divergence is a classified difference, not an expectation (SCHEMA.md section 5).
#
# A line is added in a new corpus revision, with a dated row in conformance/vectors/REVISIONS.md,
# and is removed only the same way, when the engine's cut-over corrects the difference and lists
# the correction in its changelog. Every reason names the plan that answers it.
#
# Revision 2, 2026-10-12: every difference of Broiler.JS's first engine-level run, on revision 1.
# Revision 3, 2026-10-12: every difference of Broiler.VM's first engine-level run, on revision 2.

reason vm-radix-fraction: Number::toString at a radix other than 10 gives fraction digits that do not denote the value: the expansion stops at half the gap to the next double above, computed in double arithmetic, which is not the specification's criterion. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-3-number-conversion-pilot.md, SJL-3.4 (Broiler.VM's cut-over).
reason vm-radix-integer: Number::toString at a radix other than 10 gives integer digits that do not denote the value at a magnitude of 2^53 or more: the integer part is divided by the radix in double arithmetic, which is inexact there. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-3-number-conversion-pilot.md, SJL-3.4 (Broiler.VM's cut-over).
reason vm-shortest-digits: Number::toString at radix 10 gives 16 digits that do not round-trip where the shortest digits that do are 17. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-3-number-conversion-pilot.md, SJL-3.4 (Broiler.VM's cut-over).

reason vm-parse-int-approximated: parseInt at a radix whose result the specification lets an implementation approximate gives a value other than the correctly rounded one this engine's plan commits it to (SJL-3.3). Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-3-number-conversion-pilot.md, SJL-3.4 (Broiler.VM's cut-over).
reason vm-non-decimal-rounding: A hexadecimal, octal or binary integer beyond 2^53, as a literal or a string, is not converted to the nearest double with ties to even. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-3-number-conversion-pilot.md, SJL-3.4 (Broiler.VM's cut-over).
reason vm-legacy-octal-fraction: 07.5 is read as the one literal 7.5, in sloppy and in strict code; 07 is a LegacyOctalIntegerLiteral, and the .5 that follows it makes the script a SyntaxError.
