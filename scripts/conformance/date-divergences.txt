# The Date area's classified differences, read by generate_date_vectors.py.
#
# A line `reason <key>: <text>` defines a reason. A line `<engine> <key> <id>` classifies the vector
# <id> as a divergence of <engine> for that reason: the generator writes the field
# divergence.<engine>: <text>, and a normative vector becomes a divergence. An impl-defined vector
# stays impl-defined, the field classifying the engine's difference from its own per-engine
# expectation. The vector's expected value never changes (SCHEMA.md section 5).
#
# A line is added in a new corpus revision, with a dated row in conformance/vectors/REVISIONS.md,
# and is removed only the same way, when the engine's cut-over corrects the difference and lists
# the correction. Every reason names the plan that answers it and the row of bundle sjl-6-001's
# correspondence from code (Broiler.VM's docs/evidence/sjl-6-001/correspondence.md) it confirms.
#
# Revision 15, 2026-10-15: every difference of each engine's first engine-level run on the Date area,
# at revision 14 (Broiler.JS's docs/evidence/sjl-6-001/engine/ and Broiler.VM's bundle sjl-6-001):
# 1,016 of Broiler.JS's, run in America/New_York, of which 65 also differ under UTC, and 16 of
# Broiler.VM's. A Broiler.JS vector of reason js-date-only-local or js-offset-mixed matches under UTC,
# where its run reports the divergence as no longer holding; the rule of this repository's bundle
# sjl-6-001 runs the area in both zones.
reason vm-month-float-modulo: MakeDay takes its month modulo 12 in floating point (DateFloorMod), where 1e19 - 12 x floor(1e19 / 12) rounds to 0, so a month of 1e19 is read as January, where R(m) modulo 12, taken exactly, gives May. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-6-date.md, SJL-6.4 (JSD-0072; the correspondence's row T4).
reason vm-month-day-reduced-year: Temporal.PlainMonthDay.from reduces a year past 1e9 in magnitude to 1972 + year modulo 400 before it regulates the date, so February 29 is accepted, constrained or refused by the leap-ness of a different year, where it is regulated in the year given. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-6-date.md, SJL-6.4 (JSD-0072; the correspondence's row G3).
reason vm-parse-negative-year: Date.parse reads back none of this engine's own toUTCString renderings for a negative year and answers NaN, where it must answer the time value rendered (21.4.3.2): its textual reader takes no sign on the year. Answered by Broiler.VM docs/javascript-shared-libraries/milestones/sjl-6-date.md, SJL-6.4 (JSD-0072; the correspondence's row P9).
reason js-month-saturates: MakeDay converts its month to a 64-bit integer, which saturates, so a month of 1e19 is read as 2^63 - 1 and the date falls in August, where R(m) modulo 12, taken exactly, gives May. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row T4).
reason js-setter-order: setUTCMinutes, setUTCSeconds, setUTCMonth, setHours, setMinutes, setSeconds and setMonth make the date invalid as soon as their first argument is undefined or NaN, before the later arguments are converted, so a later argument's valueOf reads getTime() as NaN, where the date is written only after every argument is converted. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row T3).
reason js-setter-outside-range: A setter whose result is a valid date outside the years 0001 to 9999 answers NaN and makes the date invalid, because the DateTimeOffset arithmetic it computes with throws, where it answers that date and stores it. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row D1).
reason js-setter-raw-date: A setter on a date this engine stores as a raw time value, outside the years 0001 to 9999, answers NaN and leaves the date unchanged, where it answers the new date and stores it. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row D2).
reason js-sentinel-instant: The instant 0001-01-01T00:00:00.000Z is this engine's invalid-date sentinel, so a date at that instant, whether a setter or the constructor makes it, is invalid: the setter answers NaN, and toUTCString answers Invalid Date, which Date.parse reads as NaN, where the time value is -62135596800000. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row D3).
reason js-iso-week-negative-year: Temporal.PlainDate's weekOfYear and yearOfWeek count the weeks of a negative ISO year with a division that truncates toward zero, so -0001-01-01 falls in week 52 rather than 53 of -2 and -0002-12-28 in week 1 of -1 rather than 53 of -2, where ISOWeekOfYear's weekday arithmetic is floored. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row G2).
reason js-parse-own-rendering: Date.parse reads back none of this engine's own toUTCString renderings for a year below 1 or above 9999 and answers NaN, where it must answer the time value rendered (21.4.3.2): the .NET formats it parses with cannot hold the year. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row P9).
reason js-date-only-local: A date-only form of the Date Time String Format for a year from 0001 to 9999 is read as local time, where it is UTC; the difference shows in a zone whose offset at that date is not zero, such as America/New_York, and not under UTC. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row P1).
reason js-offset-mixed: Local time takes the zone's base offset where a date is made from local fields, set by a local setter or read by the local getters, and the offset in force at the instant where getTimezoneOffset, toString and some of Date.parse's forms read it, so in daylight-saving time, and in the years before the zone's standard time, the local fields, the offset and the rendering disagree, where one offset, the one in force at the instant, serves them all; the difference shows in a zone with daylight-saving time or a historical offset, such as America/New_York, and not under UTC. Answered by Broiler.JS docs/roadmap/SharedLibraries.md, SJL-6.5 (the Date area's record; the correspondence's row L2).
