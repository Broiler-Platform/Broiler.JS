# A line `reason <key>: <text>` defines a reason. A line `<engine> <key> <id>` classifies the vector <id> as a
# divergence of <engine> for that reason: the generator writes the field divergence.<engine>: <text>. A normative
# vector becomes a divergence. An impl-defined one stays impl-defined: its per-engine.<engine> field is the
# expectation that engine's records commit it to, and the divergence field says the engine does not meet it yet
# (SCHEMA.md section 5). The vector's expected value never changes.
