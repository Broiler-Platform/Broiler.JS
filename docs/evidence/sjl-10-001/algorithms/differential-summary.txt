  F7 ZonedDateTime until/since: 1325297700000000000 1325239200000000000 Pacific/Apia iso8601 ZonedDateTime since largest Day smallest Day increment 1 HalfEven
       vm:  crash ArgumentOutOfRangeException
       lib: RangeError
- The case: Pacific/Apia skipped 2011-12-30. ZonedDateTime 2011-12-31T16:15+14:00 since 2011-12-31T00:00+14:00,
  largestUnit and smallestUnit day: the day window back from the receiver ends on the skipped day, which
  GetEpochNanosecondsFor (compatible) moves onto the window's start, so the window has no length and cannot
  hold the destination, which the specification asserts. Broiler.VM's RoundRelativeDuration hands the zero
  span to ExactRounding.ApplyUnsignedRoundingMode, whose ArgumentOutOfRangeException ("the denominator is not
  positive") escapes. The reference polyfill at e8cc03fc fails its own assertion ("assertion failure: day was 0
  days long", a plain Error) for since in each of the six modes tried, for the receiver's until, and for
  Duration.round and total of -PT16H15M to a day relative to the receiver; the forward until answers P1D. The library answers OutOfRange (commit cc3e3076).
- So the correspondence's row D12, "code no input reaches", does not hold for the day window: an input reaches
  it through a zone's skipped day. No other population member reached it.

Negative control
- With the library's nudge after a shifted window replaced by the polyfill's (the window's end where the
  window was shifted, which decision D7 overrules), the durations sections at scale 0.3 found 94 differences:

  and ZonedDateTime 21; and 1 each in the random round (plain and zoned) and PlainDateTime sections. The
  library was restored afterwards and rebuilt.
